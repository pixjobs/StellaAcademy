## The short version (for everyone, including younger learners)

- You do not need to tell us who you are to use Stella Academy.
- Your progress, notes and settings stay on your own device. We never see them.
- If you press **Accept** on the cookie message, Google Analytics counts which pages are visited so we can see which
  lessons help. If you press **Decline**, it does not set cookies. You can change your mind at any time.
- If you report a problem with a lesson, please do not include your name or anything about yourself. We use reports
  only to fix mistakes, and delete them after 30 days.
- We do not show adverts and we do not sell anyone's information.

## The full version

**Who we are.** Stella Academy is run by [owner's name], who decides how the information described here is used
(the "data controller"). Contact: [email address].

**What we collect, and why.**

- **When you visit.** Like any website, our hosting (Google Cloud) records each request: your IP address, your
  browser type, the page and the time. We use this to keep the site running and secure. It is deleted after 30 days.
- **Analytics, only if you accept.** With your consent, Google Analytics sets cookies and records the pages you view
  and actions such as opening an answer, along with your device type and approximate area. We use it to learn which
  lessons are useful. [Describe the before-consent behaviour once decided.] Analytics data is kept for [2 months].
- **Problem reports.** If you use "Report a problem", we keep the lesson, the kind of problem and what you wrote,
  with any email address or phone number removed, for 30 days. We use reports only to correct lessons. We do not
  link a report to you.
- **Gallery searches.** The words you search for are sent to NASA's image library to find pictures. We do not keep
  them or send them to Google Analytics.
- **Things kept on your device.** Your cookie choice, journey progress, notes and display settings are saved in your
  browser so the site remembers them. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

**Our reasons (lawful bases).** Running and securing the site, problem reports and limiting abuse: our legitimate
interest in providing accurate, safe lessons. Analytics cookies: your consent.

**Who else is involved.** Google provides our hosting, logging and analytics and acts on our instructions. NASA
pictures load directly from NASA's servers. Ko-fi is a separate site; if you follow our support link, Ko-fi's own
privacy policy applies.

**Where information is stored.** Google may process information outside the UK, under its standard data protection
terms.

**Your rights.** You can ask to see, correct or delete information about you, object to how we use it, or withdraw
consent. Because we do not collect names or accounts, we usually cannot tell which information is yours, but contact
us and we will help where we can. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

**Children.** Stella Academy is designed for ages 9 and up. We collect as little as possible, show no adverts and do
not build profiles. If we ever add accounts, children under 13 will need a parent or carer to set them up.
